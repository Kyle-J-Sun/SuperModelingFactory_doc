# v0.9.2

The screening IV on WOE bins leaves class-pure bins out of the IV sum by default. This fixes the known issue of
[0.9.1](v0.9.1.md#known-issue-screening-iv-on-woe-bins-counts-class-pure-special-value-bins). The binning, the WOE and the
scores do not change. The [upgrade guide](upgrading_0.9.md) lists every setting that reproduces earlier numbers.

!!! warning "Behavior-changing fixes (exception to RELEASING.md)"

    A patch release normally changes no numbers on healthy inputs. 0.9.2 changes the screening IV of features with a
    class-pure bin, and therefore which features the IV threshold, the correlation stage and the truncation keep. It
    ships as a patch release because the 0.9.1 IV was inflated by a number that `eps=1e-6` alone sets; this is a
    documented exception to the release policy, the same way as 0.9.1.

    | Change | What changes | Reproduce 0.9.1 with |
    |---|---|---|
    | `screen_iv_convention="guarded"` (new, the default): the screening IV on WOE bins leaves class-pure bins out of the sum (section 1) | The IV of every feature with a bin that holds no bad or no good, in `feature_screen` with `iv_use_woe_bins` (and every weighted run with an engine), in the selection stage, `ivks_summary` and the group and per-target evidence of `FeatureValidationPipeline`, in the screening of `CreditModelPipeline` on WOE bins, and in `VarExtractionInsights` / `CorrelationFilter` with a WOE engine; hence the features that `iv_threshold`, the correlation stage and `max_selected_features` keep | `screen_iv_convention="raw"` in `FeatureScreenConfig`, `FeatureValidationPipelineConfig`, `CreditModelPipelineConfig`, `VarExtractionInsights` or `CorrelationFilter`.     and the list of class-pure special-value bins (section 2), the drop reason `"iv_above_upper_sv_pure"` of the leakage
    gate (section 3; the gate drops the same features as before), the `odps` import error (section 5).

## 1. The Screening IV Leaves Class-Pure Bins Out of the Sum

The screening on WOE bins scores each bin with `woe = ln((bad share + 1e-6) / (good share + 1e-6))` and sums
`(bad share - good share) * woe`. A class-pure bin (only goods or only bads), usually a special-value (SV) bin, gets a WOE
that the `1e-6` alone sets, and up to 0.9.1 it inflated the IV:

- 5,000 rows, a special value `-999` on 150 rows that are all good (the reproduction of this release): the SV bin has a
  WOE of `-10.50` and an IV contribution of `0.38`; the feature's IV was `1.74`.
- The test frame of the 0.9.1 known issue (a sentinel on 10% of the rows that holds only bads): IV `4.02`, of which
  `3.58` came from that bin. The 0.9.1 notes rounded it to 4.03; the value is 4.0247.

`screen_iv_convention="guarded"` applies the `iv_guard` convention that `MonotoneWOEBinner`'s by-group IV already uses: a
bin with zero bads or zero goods is left out of the IV **sum**. It stays in the denominators: its rows remain in the total
bad and good counts (the `sv_total_basis="all"` basis), so the WOE and IV of every other bin are exactly those of 0.9.1.
The rule covers special-value bins and ordinary bins that `min_n_bins` kept unmerged (a near-perfect separator). As in
the binner's convention, a special-value bin that the binner smoothed (`sv_woe_smoothing="laplace"` with
`sv_smoothing_alpha > 0`) is not left out: its WOE is finite and not set by `eps`, so it counts with that WOE,
`(bad share - good share) * woe`. Smoothing is not used as the fix: `alpha` acts as a pseudo-count on the whole bin, so
its effect fades as the bin grows (an all-bad SV bin of 200 rows only goes from a WOE of 9.9 to 8.1, one of 2,000 rows
stays at 10.4).

| Frame | `iv` (guarded, the default) | `iv_raw` (0.9.1) | `iv_sv_pure` | `n_sv_pure` |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `-999` example (150 rows, all good) | 1.3610 | 1.7407 | 0.3797 | 150 |
| 0.9.1 test frame (`f_special`, selection on INS) | 0.4437 | 4.0247 | 3.5809 | 197 |

Both screening paths use one shared implementation (`Modeling_Tool/_utils/screen_iv.py`). Class purity is judged on the
quantities that the IV uses: the weighted bad and good sums on a weighted run, the counts on an unweighted one. With unit
weights both paths flag the same bins and give the same IV (tested on both frames). A bin whose bads all carry weight 0
is class-pure on the weighted path only.

The guarded IV is what the screening decides on:

- `iv_threshold` (the IV stage) and `iv_cut` of the reports;
- the IV that decides between two correlated features (`CorrelationFilter`, the weighted correlation stage);
- the ranking of `max_selected_features` and of the VIF tie-break. Up to 0.9.1 the ranking substituted the zero-cell-floored
  IV when `iv_upper_threshold` was set; under `"guarded"` it ranks on the guarded IV, under `"raw"` as in 0.9.1;
- the IV of the group-stability and per-target gates of `FeatureValidationPipeline` (`monthly_iv_min`,
  `monthly_iv_cv_max`, `per_target_iv_range`), per group on the group's own counts.

Where the setting lives:

| Object | Setting |
|---|---|
| `FeatureScreenConfig` (`feature_screen`, `screen_config_from_mapping`) | `screen_iv_convention` |
| `FeatureValidationPipelineConfig` | `screen_iv_convention` (`selection_params` and `corr_params` may repeat it; a different value there raises `ValueError`) |
| `CreditModelPipelineConfig` | `screen_iv_convention` (`feature_selection` may repeat it; a different value there raises `ValueError`) |
| `VarExtractionInsights`, `CorrelationFilter` | `screen_iv_convention` (constructor) |

The paths without WOE bins (the decision-tree IV of `VarExtractionInsights` and the weighted equal-frequency IV) already
have their own class-pure handling (the decision-tree IV zeroes such bins, the weighted IV leaves them out through
`iv_guard`) and are not affected. `CreditModelPipeline` screens on WOE bins only when `feature_selection` sets
`iv_use_woe_bins` (its default `woe_engine` is `"equal_freq"`); `FeatureValidationPipeline` uses WOE bins by default.

## 2. New Columns and the List of Class-Pure Special-Value Bins

Every screening table that shows the IV gains three columns next to `iv`:

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `iv` | The IV under `screen_iv_convention` (guarded by default) |
| `iv_raw` | The IV with every bin counted, the 0.9.1 value |
| `iv_sv_pure` | The IV contribution of the class-pure bins (special-value or ordinary) |
| `n_sv_pure` | The rows in those bins |

They appear in `FeatureScreenResult.iv_table` (which keeps `iv_weighted`, equal to `iv`), the `iv` table of the
selection summaries (`feature_selection_summary["iv"]`, `selection_summary["iv"]`, `iv_report.csv`), `ivks_summary`, and
the report of `VarExtractionInsights.get_var_analysis_report` with a WOE engine. The paths without WOE bins report
`iv_raw = iv` and NaN for `iv_sv_pure` and `n_sv_pure`.

A separate table lists every class-pure **special-value** bin (a bin that the engine flags as special, or whose rows are
all missing): `var`, `special_value`, `bin_label`, `n` (rows), `bad`, `good` (weighted sums on a weighted run),
`bad_rate`, `woe` (the WOE that the binning gives the bin, the value `apply_woe` writes), `iv` (its contribution to
`iv_raw`) and `low_count` (`n < 30`). It is meant to be handed to the strategy team as candidate rules; nothing in the
package acts on it.

| Where | Name |
|---|---|
| `feature_screen` | `result.stage_tables["iv_sv_pure_bins"]` |
| `VarExtractionInsights` | `insights.iv_sv_pure_bins` after `get_var_analysis_report` |
| `FeatureValidationPipeline` | `result.ivks_sv_pure_bins` (global rows of `ivks_summary`, with a `target` column; table `ivks_sv_pure_bins`) and `selection_summary["stage_tables"]["iv_sv_pure_bins"]` (table `selection_iv_sv_pure_bins`) |
| `CreditModelPipeline` | `feature_selection_summary["iv_sv_pure_bins"]` and `iv_sv_pure_bins.csv` |

Each table is present only when there is at least one such bin.

## 3. The Leakage Gate Keeps Its Floored IV and Labels Drops Caused by a Pure SV Bin

A special value that holds only bads can be a label leak, so the gate (`iv_upper_threshold`) does **not** switch to the
guarded IV: it keeps the zero-cell-floored IV on equal-frequency bins of 0.9.1 and drops the same features under both
conventions. When a feature exceeds the limit but would not exceed it without its class-pure special-value bins (the
floored IV recomputed on the other rows), `dropped_detail` records the reason `"iv_above_upper_sv_pure"` instead of
`"iv_above_upper"`, so that a human can review the bin. The feature is dropped all the same. A genuine leak, with or
without such a bin, keeps the reason `"iv_above_upper"` (tested on both paths: the guarded IV of the leak is 1.8, far
below the limit of 2.0, while its floored IV is 21).

## 4. Scoring Is Unchanged; Resolve Class-Pure Bins Before Training

`screen_iv_convention` changes the screening only. The WOE stored in the binning tables and used by `apply_woe` and
`transform` does not change: the `-999` bin of the example keeps its WOE of `-10.50`, and `apply_woe` gives the 0.9.1
values (tested against values recorded with 0.9.1). Existing models score identically.

!!! warning "The WOE of a class-pure bin has no statistical meaning"

    The WOE of a bin without bads or without goods is set by `eps=1e-6`: it is about `ln(1e-6 / share)`, so it grows
    with the size of the bin, not with its risk. When you build a new model, resolve every bin flagged `sv_class_pure`
    (the 0.9.1 warning, the `sv_class_pure` column of the SV table, or the lists of section 2) before training: give the
    special value a separate rule, or set `sv_small_policy="neutral"` (WOE 0) or `"merge_missing"` (the WOE of the
    `[Missing]` bin). A WOE cap is out of scope for this release.

## 5. Small Fixes

- `Modeling_Tool/Core/kDataFrame.py`: the regular expression `"\w+"` of `convert_strlist_to_list` is a raw string
  (`r"\w+"`). Python 3.12 warns about the invalid escape (`SyntaxWarning`) and a future version will reject it. No
  behavior change.
- pyodps: the `odps` extra (`pip install 'supermodelingfactory[odps]'`, declared in `pyproject.toml`) installs `pyodps`.
  Importing `Modeling_Tool.Core.ODPS_Tool` (`ODPSRunner`, `ParallelODPSManager`) without it now raises an `ImportError`
  that names the extra instead of `No module named 'odps'`. See [Installation](../installation.md).
